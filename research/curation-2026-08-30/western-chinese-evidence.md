### Sextus Empiricus

Source route: [Perseus catalog and open text route](https://atlas.perseus.tufts.edu/library/urn:cts:greekLit:tlg0544.tlg001.1st1K-eng1/)

Use basis: quotation exception; each card uses one short continuous passage, while the proposed reading unit is routed rather than bundled.

#### `sextus-opposes-appearances-judgements`

- Source text: “Scepticism is an ability, or mental attitude, which opposes appearances to judgements in any way whatsoever.”
- Locator and range: Book I, chapter 4, §8; definition of scepticism.
- Proposed reading unit: Book I, chapters 1–4, through §11, so “ability,” opposition, equipollence, suspension, and quietude remain together.
- Illustration prompt: AGORA editorial illustration, a balanced mobile with an eye-shaped translucent plate facing a small judge's scale, neither side settling, muted parchment and blue-grey, generous negative space, no portrait, no text, 4:5.

#### `sextus-suspense-neither-deny-affirm`

- Illustration prompt: AGORA editorial illustration, two unopened doors marked only by opposing abstract shapes and a resting chair exactly between them, quiet stone palette, no symbols resembling letters, no text, 4:5.

#### `sextus-quietude-opinion`

- Source text: “We assert still that the Sceptic’s End is quietude in respect of matters of opinion and moderate feeling in respect of things unavoidable.”
