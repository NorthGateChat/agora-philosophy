## Indian-philosophy evidence

### Carvaka transmission rule

The two Carvaka cards are reconstructions of a fourteenth-century Advaita compendium's hostile report, not direct quotations from a lost Carvaka work. The quotable source is what Madhava/Cowell–Gough reports. Every runtime excerpt, reading unit, and caption must preserve that reporting subject and must never label the English as “the Carvaka original.”
