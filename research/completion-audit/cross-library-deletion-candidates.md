## Same-work, same-argument overlaps

5. `popper` and `science-popper-falsifiability`
   - Both point to section 6 of *The Logic of Scientific Discovery* and teach
     the same falsifiability demarcation claim.
   - Suggested choice: keep the science card as the canonical source-led
     treatment; retain the Western card only if its philosophy-of-science
     lineage adds a visibly different question and deep reading.

6. `popper-asymmetry` and `science-popper-induction`
   - Both use Popper's white-swan asymmetry from the same work to explain why
     universal statements can be falsified but not established by accumulating
     confirming instances.
   - Suggested choice: consolidate the source explanation, then keep a second
     card only if one is explicitly about logical asymmetry and the other about
     the problem of induction.

7. `kuhn-normal-science-puzzles` and `science-kuhn-normal-science`
   - Both derive from chapters III-IV of *The Structure of Scientific
     Revolutions* and present normal science as paradigm-governed puzzle
     solving.
   - Suggested choice: keep one canonical card and move the disciplinary
     framing into its connections and deep-reading contrast.

8. `kuhn-anomaly-discovery` and `science-kuhn-anomaly-discovery`
   - Both restate the chapter VI account in which discovery emerges through
     awareness and assimilation of anomaly.
   - Suggested choice: keep the better-located source card; merge any unique
     historical example or distinction from the other before archiving it.
