## Keep despite editorial status

The review does **not** recommend removing school disagreements merely to make
the library look simpler. Rāmānuja's qualified nondualism, Madhva's real
difference, Buddhist pramāṇa, and Yogācāra should remain represented. The
recommendation is to reduce repeated multi-passage cards and improve edition
modeling, not collapse Indian philosophy into one “nondual” storyline.
