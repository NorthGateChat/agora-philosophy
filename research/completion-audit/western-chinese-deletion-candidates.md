# Western + Chinese deletion candidates

Reviewed: 2026-08-28

Scope: recommendations only. No card was deleted in the completion pass. The
Chinese library produced no deletion candidate: its 101 cards are fixed short
classical passages with distinct locators, source evidence, reading aids, and
illustrations.

## Keep despite editorial status

The completion review does **not** recommend deleting the remaining 171 Western
editorial cards merely because they are not verbatim. Many preserve important
conceptual distinctions that need either a narrower claim, a fixed translation,
or a source-language display field before source-excerpt conversion. Their
explicit retain decisions live in `content/sourceCompletionWestChinese.ts`;
the decision is a provenance label, not a judgment that short quotation is
forbidden.
