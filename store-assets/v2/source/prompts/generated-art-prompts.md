# AGORA Store Visuals V2 — Generated Art Prompts

All five source illustrations are text-free raster ingredients. Product copy and real interface captures are added later by `scripts/generate-store-assets-v2.mjs`.

## 01 — Agora threshold

Quiet public square and incomplete circular colonnade on warm ivory paper, drawn with ultra-thin graphite and charcoal lines. One distant figure enters through a break in the architecture; a single muted verdigris path guides the eye. Vast negative space for later typography. No text, logo, UI, signature, watermark, grid, or decorative concentric-circle background.

Output: `source/generated/agora-threshold.png`

## 02 — Five paths

Five distinct visual paths approach an open, broken circle while remaining recognizably different: geometric construction, flowing river line, calligraphic trace, archival fold, and scientific compass/orbit fragment. Warm ivory, graphite, charcoal, one restrained verdigris accent, large areas of silence. No text, logo, UI, portrait, signature, watermark, or political symbol.

Output: `source/generated/five-paths.png`

## 03 — Idea lineage

A blank sentence-line branches into a sparse network of marginal threads, footnote connectors, open frames, and incomplete circles, suggesting a thought's source, counterpoint, and later echo. Sophisticated manuscript-like editorial drawing, warm ivory and graphite with one muted verdigris accent, and quiet space for typography. No text, logo, UI, signature, watermark, or decorative grid.

Output: `source/generated/idea-lineage.png`

## 04 — Private keeping

A small paper fragment rests inside two protective open arcs while unrelated lines pass outside them; a tiny gate and subtle ripple suggest a private reading archive that remains permeable. Minimal graphite on warm ivory with one muted verdigris accent. No literal shield, lock, text, logo, UI, signature, watermark, or concentric background.

Output: `source/generated/private-keeping.png`

## 05 — Carry the thought

A small paper fragment unfolds into three outward paths: one settles into an archival tray, one becomes a duplicated contour, and one continues through an incomplete arch toward a distant figure. The paths imply saving, copying, and sharing without literal icons or platform logos. Warm ivory, ultra-thin graphite, charcoal, enormous negative space, and one restrained verdigris accent. No text, UI, devices, arrows, signature, watermark, or decorative grid.

Output: `source/generated/carry-the-thought.png`
