## Detailed description

The following text is ready to paste into the store listing:

```text
One page, one idea.

AGORA turns Chrome’s new tab into a quiet place for thought. Each tab opens a randomly selected study card: begin with the original text or a bilingual proposition, then continue into interpretation, argumentative context, boundaries, intellectual roots, alternative answers, and later echoes.

Five collections

• Western Philosophy
• Chinese Philosophy
• Writings of Mao Zedong
• Scientific Thought
• Indian Philosophy

Choose Across Collections to wander between traditions.

More than a quote wall

AGORA contains 1,000+ study cards, with hundreds of original minimalist line illustrations across selected entries. Cards identify the thinker, work, source clues, and the nature of the text whenever possible; direct quotations, renderings, and editorial study notes are not presented as the same thing.

Designed for everyday reading

• Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and English, with optional bilingual display
• Interpretation, context, boundaries, roots, counterpoints, and echoes
• Saved, recent, and hidden items with search and filters
• Three manuscript themes with restrained ambient motion
• Keyboard, mouse, and touch navigation
• Desktop or mobile share cards: save, copy, or use system sharing

Quiet by design. Private by default.

No account, no ads, no tracking, and no Chrome permissions. Preferences and reading lists stay on your device, and the extension works offline.

AGORA is not a substitute for primary texts. It is an invitation to read one layer deeper.
```

## Five-screen store narrative

Chrome Web Store accepts no more than five screenshots. V2 follows a complete product journey—encounter, wander, read deeper, keep, and carry—rather than presenting a checklist of screens. Real product UI must remain dominant; generated art is only a conceptual source layer or a restrained background.

### 01 — Encounter

- File: `upload/global/screenshot-01-discover-1280x800.png`
- Headline: **Meet a new idea with every new tab.**
- Supporting line: One page, one idea.
- Real UI: the full new-tab experience, a bilingual thought card, thinker, and work.
- Generated source: a very faint open threshold crossed by light, kept away from the main text.

### 02 — Wander

- File: `upload/global/screenshot-02-collections-1280x800.png`
- Headline: **Five collections. One open-ended journey.**
- Supporting line: Western · Chinese · Mao · Science · Indian
- Real UI: the current collection selector, including Across Collections.
- Generated source: five paths or incomplete archways meeting in one open space; no portraits or political symbols.

### 03 — Read deeper

- File: `upload/global/screenshot-03-read-deeper-1280x800.png`
- Headline: **Not just a quote. Follow where an idea came from—and where it led.**
- Supporting line: Text & rendering, interpretation, context, counterpoints, and echoes
- Real UI: a content-rich detail view with its interpretation structure and thought connections visible.
- Generated source: the selected card’s original line illustration, used at low contrast within the narrative edge or background.

### 04 — Keep

- File: `upload/global/screenshot-04-library-1280x800.png`
- Headline: **Keep what matters. Hide what doesn’t.**
- Supporting line: Saved, recent, and hidden thoughts stay on your device
- Real UI: the thought library with saved, recent, hidden, search, or filter states.
- Generated source: sparse bookmark, index-card, or archival-page linework rather than a literal bookshelf.

### 05 — Carry

- File: `upload/global/screenshot-05-share-1280x800.png`
- Headline: **Carry an idea into the next conversation.**
- Supporting line: Create desktop or mobile cards, then save, copy, or share
- Real UI: the share workspace, desktop/mobile selector, and the three actual actions.
- Privacy line: No account · No tracking · No permissions · Works offline
- Generated source: an abstract page extending into another space; avoid a collage of platform logos.
